# Handover privacy policy (draft; verify against the released build)

Handover stores the property records, photos, observations and reports you create in app-private storage on your device. We do not operate a server for Handover. The application is designed without ads, analytics SDKs, sign-in, or network permission; we do not receive your photos, names or inspection details.

When you choose to take a photograph, the app requests camera permission. Photos imported with Android's system picker retain their source data when available, though original metadata may be incomplete or editable. Files are shared only when you initiate export, backup or Android's share sheet. When you select a cloud storage provider or third-party sharing target, that service applies its own privacy policy.

Optional encrypted backups use a password entered on your device. The password is not stored by Handover and cannot be reset by us. Without a manual backup, uninstalling the app or losing the device may permanently lose your data.

SHA-256 digests detect changes relative to local records but do not prove picture authenticity or a trustworthy capture date. You should review sensitive details visible *inside* photographs before exporting or sharing them.

To delete records, delete an inspection from within the app when the UI feature is released, or uninstall Handover to remove its app-private storage. This policy is provisional until all implemented release features have been verified.
