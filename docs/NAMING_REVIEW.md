# Preliminary name collision review — 2026-10-02

**Handover is a working title, not cleared for commercial use.** App-store search identified:

- *Handover: Client Reports* (Mathieu Bolard, Google Play, updated July 8, 2026): local-first photo-based before/after client reporting, checklists, PDF and ZIP/CSV Pro offerings. https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.handover.clientreports
- *Handover App* (Handover App Ltd., Google Play, updated October 26, 2025): property defect inspections and shared reports. https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.maccadesign.handover.preview
- *Rental Inspection Report / ÜbergabeCheck* (InetConnector): guided apartment photos, room checklists, condition comparison and PDF. https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.inetconnector.handover

These overlapping names and product functions make **Handover** a poor name to presume unique. Keep it provisional while design and development continue. Check official trademark registers, relevant jurisdictions and app-store listings before selecting, advertising or filing the final name. A store-search result is **not** legal clearance. Do not assume screenshots or competitors' photos are licensed for incorporation into the app.

Product differentiation under development: no subscription or purchase gate after the paid download, combined vehicle/device/room templates, fully local user-driven before/after comparison, manual encrypted export. Do not claim novelty or exclusive functionality without verified evidence.
