# Product specification - Handover

**Promise:** Record initial condition, compare on return, and share a clear report.
**Monetization:** $2.99 equivalent paid Play listing, one-time purchase; no ads, subscriptions, billing SDK, hidden caps, account, or server. No INTERNET permission.
**Languages:** English default; Arabic, French, Spanish planned; full UI/localized reports and RTL must pass the gate before release.
**Templates:** Rental vehicles, devices for repair/loan, apartments with user-named rooms. One point can have multiple photos; captured, skipped, N/A, and pending are separate statuses. Snapshot template when creating an inspection.
**States:** DRAFT → BASELINE_DONE → AWAITING_RETURN → RETURN_DONE → ARCHIVED; generating reports is not a workflow state. Supports one-session PDFs with an explicit missing-return message.
**User agency:** Differences and observations are user assessments, not legal certification or automatic defect detection.
**Data locality:** Android app-private files and Room; manual user-initiated SAF backup and system report sharing.
**Non-goals:** cloud sync, AI defect analysis, OCR requirement, electronic signature, video/voice, automatic third-party sharing, guaranteed authenticity or damage detection.
**Naming:** Handover is provisional; conduct formal trademark checks across target countries and Play Store title/package checks before release. General-name overlap was not legally cleared.
